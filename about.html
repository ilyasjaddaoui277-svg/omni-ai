About OmniAI

We exist to simplify AI

Founded in 2024 by infrastructure engineers, OmniAI is on a mission to make frontier AI accessible, affordable, and effortless for every team.

Our mission

AI shouldn't require a PhD, five vendor contracts, and a dedicated infrastructure team. We believe the best AI platform is the one you never have to think about — it just routes, scales, and saves you money while you build.

Today, over 12,000 teams trust OmniAI to run mission-critical AI workloads — from startups shipping their first feature to enterprises processing millions of requests daily.
