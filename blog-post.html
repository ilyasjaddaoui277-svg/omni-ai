How Smart Routing Cuts AI Costs by 38%

SM

Sarah Mitchell

CTO at Quantix · AI infrastructure writer

Most teams pick a single flagship model and route everything through it. It is simple — and expensive. Our benchmarks across 12 models and 4 task categories show that a smart routing layer consistently outperforms any single-model strategy on both cost and quality.

The problem with one-model-fits-all

Frontier models excel at complex reasoning, but they are overkill for classification, extraction, and summarization. Paying premium per-token rates for tasks a smaller model handles flawlessly is the single largest source of wasted AI spend we see in production teams.

"Routing is not about finding the cheapest model. It is about matching task complexity to model capability — every single request."

How OmniAI routing works

Every incoming request is evaluated against three signals in under 20 milliseconds:

  • Task classification — a lightweight classifier determines complexity and required capabilities.
  • Live benchmarks — per-model quality scores updated hourly from production traffic.
  • Cost & latency windows — current pricing and response times across providers.

Results from 30 days of production traffic

Across 2.1M routed requests, smart routing reduced average cost per request by 38% while improving aggregate quality scores by 6%. Failover handling eliminated 99.7% of provider-related timeouts.

Getting started

Enable model: "auto" on any request and the router takes over. You can pin models per project, set quality floors, and review every routing decision in the analytics dashboard. Your API contract never changes.

The takeaway: stop paying flagship prices for commodity tasks. Let the router do the homework — your finance team will notice by the end of the month.
